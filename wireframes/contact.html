Say hi, send a tip, or ask us for help

Pick whichever form fits. We’ll write back, usually within two or three days.

Who reads these

Amy and Ryan, the two students who started Fundsy. Nothing here goes to a support queue or an automated inbox, and nobody outside the team sees what you send.

Know something we should share?

Scholarships, discounts, free events, a restaurant that quietly takes student IDs — if it saves a DFW student money, we want it. A student on our team checks every submission before it goes on the board.

An Instagram handle or a phone number works just as well.

We read everything, even when we can’t post it.

Ask us anything

Questions about a listing, a broken link, joining as an ambassador, or how any of this works. If you’re stuck on something and don’t know who to ask, start here.

Replies usually land within two or three days.

Partner with Fundsy

We work with DFW businesses, campus offices and nonprofits that want to reach students honestly. Spotlights are free for small businesses. Tell us who you are and what you’d like to offer students, and we’ll come back with what we can do.

Amy handles partnerships and replies personally.

Send us your resume

Upload it and one of us will read it properly and write back with notes — what’s working, what to cut, and how to describe the job you actually did. It’s free, it takes us about a week, and only the Fundsy team sees your file.

Your resume

Files are stored privately and deleted once we’ve sent you notes.

Reviews go out on Sundays.
